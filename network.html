Anycast

Announce once. Serve everywhere.

Anycast spans our three core PoPs — Las Vegas, New York and Luxembourg — so a single address answers from whichever site is closest to each visitor. Run your own miniature CDN or a globally resilient DNS without juggling a record per region. The full story lives on the Anycast page.

— FAQ

Network questions, answered.

Where are your datacenters?
Four carrier-neutral facilities: Las Vegas, Nevada for the US West; New York (Piscataway, New Jersey) for the US East; Luxembourg (Roost) for Europe; and Miami, Florida for storage and edge. The three core sites — Las Vegas, New York and Luxembourg — also act as our anycast PoPs.
Do you offer IPv6?
Yes, on every slice, dual-stack by default. We were early adopters of IPv6 long before most of the industry took it seriously, so you get a proper allocation alongside your IPv4 from day one — no waitlist, no surcharge.
Is there a looking glass or status page?
Both. Use the Looking Glass to run ping, traceroute and BGP lookups from each PoP, and the live status page to track per-location health and any maintenance. There's also a speed test with per-location test files, plus our public PeeringDB profile if you want to peer with us.
What bandwidth do I get?
1 Gbps unmetered on every standard slice — no transfer caps and no overage bills. High-volume slices run at 1000 Mbps with unlimited transfer. Whatever you're pushing, the meter isn't running.
