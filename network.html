— FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where are BuyVM's datacenters located?
We run three core datacenters: Las Vegas, Nevada (US West); New Jersey in the Dupont Fabros facility in Piscataway (US East); and Roost, Luxembourg (Europe). We also operate in Miami, Florida, used for Block Storage Slabs.
Do you have a Miami location?
Yes. Miami, Florida is currently used for our Block Storage Slabs and serves as an additional region. Our three core compute locations remain Las Vegas, New Jersey (Piscataway) and Luxembourg.
Do you offer Anycast and IPv6?
Yes. Our Anycast network spans Las Vegas, New Jersey (Piscataway) and Luxembourg, letting you build a global CDN-style footprint from a single VPS. IPv6 is available across our locations.
How much bandwidth do I get?
Standard Dedicated KVM Slices come with unmetered bandwidth, and every location includes free private internal networking. We also include free DNS hosting across four US locations.
