— FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I buy just a Block Storage Slab?
No — a Slab must be paired with a Dedicated KVM Slice in the same facility to be used. There's no standalone option today. We may add an Object Store product down the line, but not at this time.
How much do Block Storage Slabs cost?
Slabs are sold in 256 GB chunks at $1.25/month. That works out to $5/month for 1 TB and just $50/month for a full 10 TB Slab — no per-IOPS surcharges.
How large can a Slab be, and how many can I attach?
Each Slab can be up to 10 TB in size, and you can attach up to 8 Slabs to a single Dedicated KVM Slice — up to 80 TB of block storage on one server.
Which locations can I buy Block Storage Slabs in?
Slabs are available in Las Vegas, Nevada; New York, New York; Miami, Florida; and Roost, Luxembourg. Your Slab and its KVM Slice need to live in the same facility.
Can I resize my Slab later?
You can upgrade a Slab up to the 10 TB maximum at any time. Downgrading isn't possible — you'd order a new, smaller Slab and transfer your data across to it yourself.
Can I move my Slab between servers?
Yes. From the Stallion control panel you can migrate a Slab between KVM Slices in the same facility. Migrations are entirely "hot" — there's no need to power off or reboot the server for the volume to reattach.
