Our story

Built by people who run servers, for people who run servers.

We started in 2010 with a simple, slightly stubborn idea: a real virtual server — your own kernel, full root, no asterisks — shouldn't cost a fortune or require a sales call. While most of the market was either expensive or oversold, we kept building honest infrastructure at honest prices, and people noticed.

BuyVM has always been run by developers and technology enthusiasts rather than business-first management. That's not a slogan — it's why we shipped affordable DDoS protection when "DDoS protection" still meant a five-figure invoice, why we're the only affordable VPS host offering Anycast IPs, and why we turned on IPv6 long before most of the industry could spell it. Somewhere in there we also built Stallion, our own control panel, which keeps getting voted the best one out there.

We're a division of Cloudzy now, with more network and reach behind us — but the ethos hasn't changed. Same nerds. Same flat prices. Same touch of insanity that made us put a 10 TB storage slab next to a $2 slice and call it a day.

— What we believe

A few opinions we refuse to outgrow.

Affordable, not cheap

There's a difference, and we live in it. We cut cost through efficiency — denser hardware, smarter networking, less overhead — never by cutting the corners you'd actually feel. You get less on the invoice and more in the slice.

Built by engineers

We're a company of developers and technology enthusiasts, not business-first management. Decisions get made by the people who run the network, which is why the product feels like it was built by someone who'd actually use it. (It was.)

Privacy first

Your server is your business. We've accepted cryptocurrency for years, we don't ask for your life story, and we don't sell what little we do keep. Privacy isn't a premium tier here — it's the default setting.

Prices that don't creep

The $2 slice we sold years ago is still $2. No annual "adjustments," no loyalty penalty for sticking around, no surprise line items. If the price moves, it tends to move in your favor — more resources for the same bill.

— Firsts

Things we did before it was cool.

A short list of moments where the rest of the affordable-hosting world was running a few years behind. We have the highly trained nerds; we may as well use them.

Affordable DDoS protection

We pioneered always-on filtering at a price normal humans could pay — back when surviving an attack otherwise meant a four-figure monthly invoice. Today it's $3/mo on any IP.

Anycast, the affordable way

We're still the only affordable VPS host offering Anycast IPs — the kind of global, latency-killing routing that used to be reserved for companies with their own NOC and a much bigger budget.

Early to IPv6

We've shipped IPv6 on every slice since long before 95% of the competition got around to it. No waitlist, no add-on fee — just an address space that was clearly going to matter.

The Stallion panel

Rather than license someone else's control panel, we built our own — deploy, snapshot, rescue and rebuild in a click. It keeps getting voted the best panel on the market, which we try not to be smug about.

Storage that's nearly free

Then we strapped multi-terabyte Block Storage Slabs onto our slices at a fraction of what the big vendors charge — up to 95% cheaper — because per-gigabyte gouging was never going to be our thing.

Humans on support, 24/7

Round-the-clock support staffed by technical experts — the highly trained nerds who actually built and run the platform, not a tier-one script reader reaching for the reboot button.
