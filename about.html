— Our story

Built by people who run servers, for people who run servers.

BuyVM started in 2010 with a stubborn idea: that fast, reliable virtual servers shouldn't cost a fortune, and that you shouldn't have to talk to a sales script to get one. We've kept the lights on every year since — growing from a hopeful startup into a stable, sustainable business while keeping the same small, opinionated team at the wheel.

We aren't accountants and business-school folks running fancy spreadsheets that happen to be pointed at a hosting company. We're technologists, and we're proud of it. The people who build the platform are the people who answer your tickets, and the features we ship are the ones we'd want for our own projects. That's how you end up with a control panel we actually like using and a network we'd happily run our own workloads on.

We keep the lineup small on purpose — a handful of products tuned to perform at their peak, with uptime as the goal rather than an afterthought. Less catalog padding, more attention on the things you actually deploy.

— What we believe

A few things we refuse to compromise on.

Fourteen years in, the principles haven't changed. They're the reason customers stick around — and the reason we can look at our own invoices without wincing.

Affordable, not cheap — there's a difference

We hate the word "cheap." Calling a service cheap means corners were cut somewhere to drag the price down. We do the opposite — we charge less for more resources. Same engineering, smaller invoice.

Built by engineers — not accountants

When you reach out, you're talking to people who actually live in the VPS industry, not a quarterly-earnings algorithm wearing a hosting-company costume. We're technologists and proud of it.

Privacy matters — life after Snowden

In a world of constant surveillance, privacy still counts. We run secure systems with staff trained to handle accounts properly, offer high-privacy hosting in Luxembourg, and accept Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash, Ethereum, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Monero and many more.

Customers first — 24/7

Customers matter, first and foremost. It's always work time somewhere, so we staff support around the clock with highly trained nerds who'd rather solve your problem than route you through a phone tree.

— Firsts

We didn't follow the pack. We set the pace.

"Founding fathers" isn't just a tagline. A lot of the features the rest of the affordable-VPS market now treats as standard, we shipped first.

Pioneered affordable DDoS

We brought serious, always-on DDoS protection to the affordable end of the market — back when filtering on a budget VPS simply wasn't a thing anyone offered.

First affordable VPS with Anycast

We remain the only VPS provider to offer Anycast IP addresses on our VPS services — route every visitor to the nearest location and build a global presence without a global budget.

Early IPv6 — before 95% of the market

We rolled out IPv6 ahead of more than 95% of the VPS world. While others were still hoarding IPv4, our customers were already building on the modern internet.

Stallion — voted best panel

Our in-house Stallion control panel does everything you need to run your VPS — voted best panel on the market by its own developers. It's fully SolusVM-compatible via the same API, and it isn't for sale: it's ours, and it blazes the path others follow.
