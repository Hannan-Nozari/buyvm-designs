— FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Stallion?
Stallion is BuyVM's custom, in-house control panel for managing your virtual servers — the result of more than five years of VPS experience, and under constant development. Start, stop and reboot with a click; monitor CPU, RAM, bandwidth and disk in real time; configure failover/floating IPs, assign IPv6 & Anycast IPs, set reverse DNS; and deploy from 100+ OS templates. It isn't for sale — it's part of the BuyVM stable of solutions other hosts follow.
Is DirectAdmin free?
Yes — a DirectAdmin website control-panel license is included free with all Dedicated KVM Slice plans, so you can manage sites, databases and email without being a full-time sysadmin. DirectAdmin recommends at least 1 GB of RAM to run comfortably, and licenses are restricted to BuyVM IP addresses.
Do you have an API?
Yes. Stallion is fully compatible with SolusVM and is accessible via the same API methods, so your existing SolusVM scripts and tooling keep working without changes.
What's the difference between snapshots and backups?
Snapshots are instant restore points you create yourself — 5 free per location with every plan — perfect before a big software-stack update or OS upgrade, and great for cloning a template. Backups are automatic: an optional nightly addon at 10% of the base price (or $0.50/month on the 512 MB & 1 GB plans), taken every night with 7 days retained.
Do you offer managed databases?
Yes. Offloaded, managed SQL database servers are available in every datacenter for $1.00/month. They run MySQL on pure SSD, are tuned to handle thousands of queries per second, and are accessible over your private IP — so you incur no bandwidth charges.
