— How it works

One IP. The nearest server, every time.

With a working anycast configuration, the same IP address is announced from all three locations at once. Each visitor's traffic takes the shortest path to whichever PoP is closest to them — European visitors land in Luxembourg, United States visitors go to Las Vegas or New Jersey depending on their location, and Asia routes to Las Vegas.

By serving content closer to the visitor, latency drops and throughput rises. Both add up to a happy visitor with content that loads fast and properly.

Keep voice-over-IP latency low by terminating calls at the closest PoP — clearer audio with fewer drops and less jitter.

— FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Anycast?
Anycast is a network routing standard. Historically it has been implemented only in very large companies and at great expense. Anycast puts your visitors on the server closest to them, which results in better site performance and throughput.
Why use Anycast?
Anycast speeds up your sites and other services by putting visitors on the server closest to them. The closest-server choice means lower latency and higher throughput. It's ideal for Content Distribution Networks (CDNs) — and works just as well for DNS, game servers and voice-over-IP.
Where does BuyVM have Anycast Points-of-Presence (PoPs)?
Anycast requires VPS instances — either KVM or OpenVZ — in all BuyVM locations: Las Vegas, Nevada, USA (West Coast); Piscataway, New Jersey, USA (East Coast); and Roost, Luxembourg, Europe.
How much do Anycast IP addresses cost?
Anycast is free for all BuyVM VPS customers. Your total cost is three VPS purchases, one per location — and it works even with our popular 128 MB annual plans. For added redundancy, order additional services in each location and enable IP failover so your anycast IP fails over automatically in case of maintenance.
How do I configure my Anycast IP address?
Anycast is easily configured via our Stallion control panel. Just follow the how-to on our wiki.
Is DDoS protection available?
Yes. DDoS protection is available for $5.00/month per IP address. Please contact support to request DDoS protection be enabled on the IP addresses of your choice.
