— How it works

One IP. Many locations.

Anycast announces the same IP address — or an entire prefix — from every one of your Points of Presence at once. When a request comes in, the network's own routing tables hand it to whichever PoP is closest in network terms. Shorter path, fewer hops, lower latency, more throughput. No DNS tricks, no client-side guesswork.

Set it up once, deploy a slice in each region, and your front door is everywhere at the same time.

  • ✓Nearest-PoP routing — visitors reach the geographically closest server with no extra config on their end.
  • ✓One prefix, announced everywhere — the identical IP space lights up in all three locations simultaneously.
  • ✓DDoS-protected GRE tunnels — documented in the wiki, so clean traffic keeps flowing under attack.
  • ✓Configured in Stallion — turn it on from the same control panel that runs your slices.

— FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Anycast really free?
Yes — there's no charge for the anycast network itself, the route announcements, or the DDoS-protected GRE tunnels. The only thing you pay for is the VPS instances you run, one per Point of Presence. That's the whole bill.
How many locations can I announce from?
Three: Las Vegas, Nevada (US West); Piscataway, New Jersey (US East); and Roost, Luxembourg (Europe). Together they cover both US coasts and continental Europe — enough to put most of the western internet a short hop from your service.
Do I really need three servers?
To get the benefit of anycast, yes — one slice in each PoP so the same IP can answer from all three. You can start smaller and grow into it, but a prefix announced from a single location is just a normal server with extra steps. The point is having a presence everywhere at once.
What's a GRE tunnel?
A GRE tunnel is an encapsulated link that carries your anycast traffic between the network edge and your slice — and on BuyVM it can run through our DDoS filtering, so clean traffic keeps flowing even under attack. Setup is documented in the wiki at wiki.buyvm.net and configured through Stallion.
Can I use my own IP space?
You can either announce a prefix we provide or bring your own, provided you have the authorization to advertise it from our network. Either way you announce it from all three PoPs through Stallion — check the wiki for the current bring-your-own-prefix requirements.
