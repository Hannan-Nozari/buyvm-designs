The system,
itemised.

Things that should cost extra elsewhere. They don’t here. The list is short on purpose.

— Module 01 / Defence

DDoS filter,
included.

Layer-3 and Layer-4 mitigation on every IP we issue, free, for every plan including the $2 Piglet. Same equipment we use to keep our own panel up — pointed at your slice.

— Module 02 / Bandwidth

One gigabit,
unmetered.

1 Gbps in, 1 Gbps out, per IP. No bandwidth meter. We measured the link, not your packets.

— Module 03 / Virtualisation

Real KVM.
Real root.

Your kernel, your iptables, your bad ideas. Not a container with a marketing layer.
