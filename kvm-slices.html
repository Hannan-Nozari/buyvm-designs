— FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between fair-share and dedicated cores?
On fair-share plans (Slice 512–2048) your vCPU shares time fairly with neighbours on the same node — great for typical web workloads. From Slice 4096 up, your core is dedicated, so you get consistent performance for CPU-bound work like databases and builds.
Can I really cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the Stallion control panel or your billing area — no retention calls, no win-back emails. Monthly plans simply stop at the end of the term; we don't lock you in.
How fast does a slice deploy?
Most slices provision and boot in under a minute after checkout. You'll get root credentials in the panel and can SSH in straight away, or use cloud-init to bootstrap automatically.
Do you offer IPv6?
Every slice includes IPv6 — we were early adopters, long before most of the industry. You get 1 IPv4 plus an IPv6 allocation by default; additional IPv4s are available on request.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Credit/debit cards, PayPal, and a range of cryptocurrencies. We've supported crypto for years as part of our privacy-first approach.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes — upgrade RAM, CPU and storage from the panel. You can also attach Block Storage Slabs up to 10 TB to any slice in the same facility without downtime.
