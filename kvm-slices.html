— FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between fair-share and dedicated cores?
Slices 512–2048 share CPU time fairly across the node (fair share) — ideal for typical web workloads. From Slice 4096 up, your core is dedicated for consistent, CPU-bound performance. We never oversell, so there are no noisy-neighbour issues either way.
Is DirectAdmin really included free?
Yes — a DirectAdmin license is now included with all Dedicated KVM Slice plans at no additional cost. DirectAdmin recommends at least 1 GB of RAM to run comfortably. Licenses are restricted to BuyVM IP addresses only.
How does pricing compare to AWS, DigitalOcean, Linode or Vultr?
For a comparable 4 GB plan, BuyVM is $15/month versus roughly $20–$40/month at the big providers — about 62.5% less than the closest competitor, with unmetered bandwidth and a dedicated CPU core included.
Do you offer snapshots and backups?
Every plan includes 5 free snapshots per location. Optional nightly backups are available at 10% of the plan price (or $0.50/month on the 512 MB and 1 GB plans), with 7 days of backups retained.
Which operating systems can I run?
Choose from 100+ one-click templates — Debian, Ubuntu, the RedHat family, FreeBSD/OpenBSD/NetBSD, Windows, OpenVPN-AS, the full TurnKey Linux lineup and more — or bring your own ISO. KVM also supports custom kernels.
What if my plan is sold out?
BuyVM stock is limited and often sells out. When it does, you can deploy an equivalent VPS instantly on our sister company Cloudzy.
